Screaming From The Corporation — The Corporate Warrior Anthology

Released January 1, 2027

日本語 | English

EMJ初期から続くフラッグシップ・シリーズ、Corporate Warrior。その全楽曲を収録したスペシャルアルバム。

企業で働く者たちに浴びせられる罵倒と理不尽。変わり続ける社会、次々と押し寄せる新たな波。それでも、時代が変わっても揺らぐことのない信念を胸に、Corporate Warriorは立ち上がる。

折れるな。迷うな。自分の信念を貫け。

熱い魂を持つCorporate Warriorの背中を追いかけろ。

新曲「Keep the Faith」「Actions Speak Louder」を収録。

日本語 | English

Corporate Warrior, the flagship series that has been at the heart of EMJ since its early days. This special album brings together every song from the series.

The abuse and unreasonable demands hurled at those who work for a company. A society that never stops changing, with new waves crashing down one after another. Yet no matter how the times may change, Corporate Warrior rises, holding firmly to an unshakable faith in his own convictions.

Do not break. Do not hesitate. Stand by what you believe. Follow the path of the Corporate Warrior, driven by a burning soul.

Featuring two new songs: "Keep the Faith" and "Actions Speak Louder."
